# Chrome Web Store – Listing

## Name
Dopablock – Screen-Time Control for Feeds

## Short description (≤ 132 chars)
Track, limit or block feeds like the TikTok FYP, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts – per section, not whole sites.

## Kurzbeschreibung (DE)
Messen, begrenzen oder sperren: TikTok-FYP, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts & Co. – pro Bereich statt ganzer Seiten.

## Description
Dopablock is a screen-time app for your browser. Instead of blocking all of TikTok or YouTube, you choose exactly which parts you want to control:

• TikTok: For You, Following, Explore, LIVE
• Instagram: Reels, Feed, Explore, Stories
• YouTube: Shorts (incl. shelves and tab), Home feed, suggested videos, comments
• Snapchat Spotlight, Facebook Reels/Watch, X/Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Twitch, Netflix, Discord
• Your own rules for any website or sub-page

Three modes per section: Track (just measure), Limit (daily allowance) or Block. Blocked pages stay visible but blurred behind a calm info card.

Willpower tools: schedules (e.g. Mon–Fri 09–17), focus sessions, "breathe first" delay before overrides, optional PIN, strict mode, daily goal with streaks.

Private by design: everything stays in your browser. No account, no tracking, no remote code. Optional sync of rules/settings through your browser account.

## Beschreibung (DE)
Dopablock ist eine Screen-Time-App für den Browser. Statt ganze Seiten zu sperren, legst du fest, welche Bereiche du kontrollieren willst – etwa nur den TikTok-FYP, Instagram Reels oder YouTube Shorts. Pro Bereich: Messen, Limit oder Sperren. Gesperrte Seiten bleiben unscharf sichtbar hinter einer ruhigen Info-Karte. Dazu Zeitpläne, Fokus-Sessions, „Durchatmen“-Wartezeit, optionale PIN, Strikter Modus und Tagesziel mit Serien. Alles bleibt lokal in deinem Browser.

## Category
Productivity

## Single purpose (for review)
Lets users track, limit or block selected websites and sections of websites to manage their own screen time.

## Permission justifications
- **Host access (http/https):** needed to compare the current page address with the user's rules and to show the block overlay / hide elements on matching pages. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **storage:** save rules, settings and local usage statistics.
- **alarms:** refresh the toolbar badge once per minute.
- **favicon:** show site icons in the popup and settings.
